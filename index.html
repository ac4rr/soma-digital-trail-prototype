Day 15 of 21

“Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.”

Philippians 4:6-7
Reflection

Anxiety often feels like an uninvited guest that refuses to leave. It demands our attention, pulling our focus away from the present moment and into a future we cannot control. This passage doesn't offer a platitude; it offers a practical exchange.

When we bring our requests to God with gratitude, we are actively shifting our perspective. We acknowledge the reality of our concerns while simultaneously anchoring ourselves in what is already good.

Application

Identify one specific worry that has been occupying your mind today. Instead of ruminating on the potential outcomes, vocalize it.

Write it down, and next to it, list three things you are currently grateful for. Notice if this simple act of re-framing creates even a small amount of breathing room in your chest.

Prayer

“God, I release my tight grip on tomorrow. Help me to trade my anxiety for your peace, trusting that you are present in the details of my life. Amen.”
